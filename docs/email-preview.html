Emails sent by the FC Photography Lab system

These are the real templates. All emails about one booking reach each person in one Gmail conversation: the first email starts it, and later steps come as replies. The red line at the top of each email says which step it is. In TEST mode everything goes to fc.jodhpur@nift.ac.in, with the real recipient in the subject.
